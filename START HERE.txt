Welcome to your Clinic Content Engine!

You can't break anything. If you get stuck, just tell Claude what you see.

What you need first
- A paid Claude plan (Pro or above) and the Claude app on your computer:
  claude.ai/download
- About 3 GB of free space, and a few minutes for the first setup.


GETTING STARTED ON YOUR MAC
1. It's a big download (about 200 MB), so give it a few minutes.
   Double-click it if it hasn't opened already. You'll get a
   folder called "Clinic Content Engine".
2. Drag that folder into your Documents folder.
3. Open the Claude app, click "Code", choose "Clinic Content Engine" from your
   Documents, and type:   set me up
4. Claude does the rest. Everything your engine needs is already inside,
   so there's nothing to install and no password to type.


WHEN YOU'RE SET UP
Say:   plan my month
Your Starter Plan is already inside your engine: your first 30 days of
posts, made for your treatments. There's nothing to paste. If you join the
monthly content plans, each new month's plan comes with a code in the
members' classroom: type the code into Claude. That's all.
Other things you can say any time: make my reels, make my talking heads,
make my posts, make my memes, show me my planner, show me the reel lookbook,
show me my brand book, help.
